# BACKGROUND — SCP: Class of '99 (Agent West) v6.0

For `NEW ENGINE v6.0`. **Round-0 truth**; once play begins this file is history and the Ledger changes instead. **Player: set only the `[UNASSIGNED]` entries before starting.** The GM withholds `locked_case_truths`, undiscovered NPC knowledge and hidden faction information during play.

**Undefined parts:** anything this background leaves undefined is the AI GM's to develop in play, consistent with its setting anchors, SCP canon and established facts (engine I3, §16.6) — never a gap to wait on, refuse over, or expect the engine to fill.

Design: SCP Foundation, present day, played light. Ridiculous anomalies that make you sweat and think, real fights where the canon allows them, office comedy, staff lore and perks, and a rookie's life between jobs. **Not dark, not rushed into work, no world-ending plot.** Work arrives at a human pace with downtime between. Every quest has a clear finish; no quest is a 50-round investigation fog. Tone and structure partly borrowed from *Control* (a shifting HQ that opens up, side jobs that are rules-puzzles with a fight attached, sudden site alerts, deadpan memos) — **no Control characters, names or places**.

### Before starting

- Player sets `[UNASSIGNED]` fields: West's given name, age (24–30), gender, appearance, and sidearm name. "West" is the surname everyone uses.
- West starts as a trained field agent, Level 2 clearance, T1 skills only. No anomalous gear, no MTF rank, no special allies beyond those below.
- Freeze as Round-0 truth: no clock moves, reveals or belief changes during setup.

### SCP primer for players new to SCP / 给不了解SCP的玩家的入门说明

*Player-safe: no spoilers. Read before starting. / 无剧透，开局前阅读。*

**What is SCP? / SCP是什么？**
SCP is a huge online shared-fiction project. In its world, a secret organisation called the **SCP Foundation** finds strange things that break the laws of nature (called **anomalies**), locks them away, and keeps the public from ever knowing. Its motto: **Secure. Contain. Protect.** Each anomaly gets a number (SCP-173, SCP-999…) and a file that reads like a dry government report — that dry tone is half the joke.
SCP是一个大型网络共创虚构项目。在这个世界里，一个叫 **SCP基金会** 的秘密组织负责寻找违反自然规律的怪东西（称为 **异常**），把它们关起来，并确保普通人永远不知道。座右铭：**控制、收容、保护**。每个异常都有编号（如SCP-173、SCP-999），并配一份像政府报告一样干巴巴的档案——这种一本正经的语气本身就是笑点的一半。

**In this game / 在本游戏中：** the Foundation hides behind a dull WHO programme, the "Special Containment Programme". You play **Agent West**, a brand-new field agent on 90 days' probation. The tone is light: weird jobs, office life, funny coworkers, some real fights.
基金会的公开身份是世卫组织下一个不起眼的"特殊收容计划"。你扮演刚入职、处于90天试用期的外勤特工 **韦斯特**。整体基调轻松：奇怪的任务、办公室日常、搞笑同事，偶尔有真正的战斗。

**Key words / 关键词**
- **Anomaly / 异常** — anything that breaks normal reality. / 任何违反正常现实的事物。
- **Containment / 收容** — keeping an anomaly locked up and secret. / 把异常关好并保密。
- **Object class / 项目等级** — how hard it is to keep in a box, *not* how dangerous it is. / 表示"多难关住"，*不是*"多危险"。
  - **Safe** — lock it in a box and it stays put. / 锁进盒子就老实待着。
  - **Euclid** — not sure what it will do; anything alive or thinking is at least Euclid. / 不确定会怎样；有生命或会思考的至少是Euclid级。
  - **Keter** — easily escapes the box. / 很容易逃出盒子。
  - **Thaumiel** — it *is* the box (used to contain other anomalies; top secret). / 它*就是*盒子（用来收容别的异常，绝密）。
- **Clearance Level / 权限等级** — what you are allowed to *know*, not your rank. West has Level 2. / 决定你能*知道*什么，不是职位高低。韦斯特是2级。
- **Field agent / 外勤特工** — the Foundation's eyes and ears: investigates, confirms, calls in the specialists. / 基金会的耳目：调查、确认，然后呼叫专业团队。
- **MTF (Mobile Task Force) / 机动特遣队** — specialist squads sent where needed. Each has a code and a nickname. / 派往各地的专业小队，每支都有代号和外号。
- **Amnestic / 记忆删除剂** — a drug that erases memories; used on witnesses. / 消除记忆的药物，用于目击者。
- **D-class / D级人员** — prisoners used for dangerous tests. / 用于危险测试的囚犯。
- **O5 Council / O5议会** — the 13 anonymous leaders at the very top. / 最高层的13位匿名领导。
- **Containment breach / 收容失效** — something got out. / 有东西跑出来了。
- **Groups of Interest / 相关组织** — outside groups that know about anomalies (dealers, artists, cults). / 知道异常存在的外部组织（商人、艺术团体、教派等）。

**Your people / 你的人**
- **MTF Omega-99 "Class of '99" / 机动特遣队Omega-99"九九届"** — a small odd-jobs squad for low-priority weird stuff worldwide. / 一支专接全球低优先级怪事的小队。
- **The 1999 joke / 1999梗** — the squad's veterans, born in the 1990s, claim they secretly saved the world as kids in 1999 and swore never to tell, so everyone younger owes them coffee. West was born too late and never hears the end of it. / 队里的九零后老鸟坚称自己小时候在1999年偷偷拯救过世界并发誓保密，所以所有更年轻的人都欠他们一杯咖啡。韦斯特出生太晚，被调侃个没完。

**Staff perks / 员工福利** — some harmless Safe-class items can be booked for staff morale with **Form MU-2**, which takes a few days. / 一些无害的Safe级物品可以用 **MU-2表** 申请来提振士气，审批要几天。
- **SCP-999** — a friendly orange jelly blob that tickles and cheers people up. / 友善的橙色果冻团，会挠痒逗人开心。
- **SCP-294** — a coffee machine that pours any liquid you name (staff may only order normal drinks). / 能倒出你说出名字的任何液体的咖啡机（员工只能点正常饮料）。
- **SCP-458** — a pizza box that never runs out. / 永远吃不完的披萨盒。
- **SCP-261** — a vending machine with strange snacks, paid in Japanese yen. / 卖奇怪零食的自动售货机，要投日元。
- **SCP-131** — two little teardrop-shaped "eye pods" that follow people around like puppies. / 两个像小狗一样跟着人跑的水滴形"眼仔"。

**Old-timer stories / 老员工的传说** — famous SCPs people joke about in the cafeteria. You won't be sent to these. / 食堂里大家拿来开玩笑的著名SCP，你不会被派去处理它们。
- **SCP-173** — a statue that moves when nobody is looking. "Don't blink." / 没人看时就会移动的雕像。"别眨眼。"
- **SCP-682** — a huge reptile that hates everything and cannot be killed. / 痛恨一切、杀不死的巨型爬行动物。
- **SCP-096** — "the Shy Guy"; seeing its face makes it hunt you. / "害羞的人"，看到它的脸它就会追杀你。
- **SCP-055** — something nobody can remember anything about. / 一个谁都记不住任何细节的东西。
- **SCP-914** — a clockwork machine that "refines" whatever you put in. / 能把放进去的东西"精炼"的发条机器。
- **SCP-500** — pills that cure any disease; never handed out. / 能治愈一切疾病的药丸，从不外发。

**Anomalies you may meet on the job (no spoilers) / 工作中可能遇到的异常（无剧透）**
- **SCP-1999 "Loewes"** — the name "Loewes" appears handwritten on museum objects; whoever holds one feels it belongs to someone else. / "Loewes"的手写名字会出现在博物馆物品上，拿着的人会觉得它属于别人。
- **SCP-4711** — a 7-Eleven in Malaysia where your favourite snack always vanishes and the toilet is always locked. / 马来西亚一家7-Eleven：你最爱的零食总会消失，厕所永远锁着。
- **SCP-4048** — an invisible alien ship that abducts forklift drivers in their sleep to quiz them about forklifts. / 一艘隐形外星飞船，会在叉车司机睡觉时把人抓去考叉车知识。
- **SCP-3947** — five living rubber ducks who act like cartoon gangsters, with anvils and frying pans; nobody gets truly hurt. / 五只像卡通黑帮一样的活橡皮鸭，会用铁砧和平底锅打人；但没人会真的受伤。
- **SCP-4557** — five raccoons stacked in a trench coat who think they are a noir detective. / 五只叠在风衣里、自以为是黑色电影侦探的浣熊。
- **SCP-3761** — very smart squirrels preparing for a huge squirrel war decades from now; they carve messages in tree bark. / 极聪明的松鼠们，正在为几十年后的一场松鼠大战做准备，会在树皮上刻字。
- **SCP-3683** — giant hostile pigeons that burst into ordinary pigeons when beaten, then join back together. / 巨型凶猛鸽子，被打败时会炸散成普通鸽子，之后再合体。
- **SCP-4968** — deer in one New Jersey county that organise to ram cars on purpose. / 新泽西某县的鹿，会有组织地故意撞车。
- **SCP-3870** — a boy in a coma whose double rides a bike and attacks ice-cream workers. / 一个昏迷的男孩，他的分身骑着自行车袭击卖冰淇淋的人。
- **SCP-1295 "Meg's Diner"** — four grumpy old men who eat pie at a highway diner every day; the Foundation must make sure nothing ever disturbs them. Staff say they are the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. / 四个每天在公路边餐馆吃派的暴躁老头，基金会必须确保没有任何事打扰他们。员工们说他们就是天启四骑士。

### GM rule — explain on first sight / 主持规则：首次出现就解释


### SCP logic the GM must keep (canon-checked)

```text
CLEARANCE ≠ RANK  levels 0–5 control what you may know, need-to-know only;
  field agents and most researchers are Level 2; MTF operatives and senior staff
  Level 3; Site Directors / MTF commanders Level 4; O5 Level 5. Having a level
  does not clear you for a project; being read in does.
FIELD AGENT  eyes and ears: undercover or embedded (police, hospitals, local
  services) or a suit who investigates. Confirms an anomaly, then calls a
  containment team. Not a soldier; may carry a sidearm.
WHO FIGHTS  security officers (site guards), tactical response teams, Mobile
  Task Forces (specialist units, often veterans). West fights when attached to
  them, when a breach reaches him, or in self-defence.
OBJECT CLASS = difficulty of containment, not danger. Safe: lock it in a box and
  it stays. Euclid: unsure (anything alive or sapient is at least Euclid). Keter:
  escapes the box easily. Thaumiel: is the box. Neutralized / Explained /
  Decommissioned as canon.
PERSONNEL CLASS  A (never near anomalies) · B (local essentials) · C (direct
  access to non-hostile anomalies; West) · D (expendable test subjects; treated
  as people, amnestics rather than casual death) · E (exposed, quarantined until
  cleared).
AMNESTICS  witnesses are given amnestics (Class A/B/C by strength); cover stories
  are routine (gas leaks, avian flu, renovation).
O5  thirteen members, numbered, never deal with day-to-day containment.
ETHICS COMMITTEE  real oversight: it limits what may be done to sapient or living
  anomalies (e.g. SCP-4968 termination rules).
WHO COVER  on paper the Foundation works through the WHO "Special Containment
  Programme" (gives the acronym and a reason to appear at odd outbreaks). WHO
  leadership knows only the cover. In practice the Foundation answers to the O5.
```

### Day 90 — chapter break, not an ending

West's probation review falls on Day 90 (`probation_review` quest). The review scene runs fully: the board weighs everything West actually did (every cover identity included), credit and grievance alike (engine §14.3 REVIEW). **The campaign does not end there.** After the scene, the GM prompts the player: *continue as is, or extend* — an extension is a new chapter built by the chapter rebase (engine §16.8) from this chapter's final state (new assignments, new SCPs, threads carried over). The 1999 folder may sit on the review table; opening it is the player's choice.

### Pressure and quest endings (setting-specific; engine §13.5 and §14 govern the rest)

- `darrow_scheme`: retires when Darrow is exposed, caught, bought off, or completes his buy; a completed buy leaves a recoverable trail, never a lost cause.
- `stag_network`: retires when the network's coordinator is captured or dead, or the herd network disperses; scattered cells stay as local trouble, not a new pressure.
- `pigeon_breakout`: local; retires when every escaped instance is killed, netted or recombined-then-killed, or crosses into a mating that MTF Gamma-119 takes over.
- `rosa_transfer`: ordinary life; retires when the request is approved, withdrawn or refused.
- Only `orientation_week` is a quest at Round 0; Site-19 life between jobs is ordinary play, not a quest. Other quests open when Rosa assigns them (work source pace) or a site alert drags West in.

## Setting notes (GM-facing)

- **Pace.** Rosa assigns one job every 4–6 days; downtime between is real play (perks, lore, cards with Leo, site alerts). Never chain jobs back to back unless the player asks.
- **Quest order.** Any order fits. Suggested first three: Night Shift, Loewes File, Trench-Coat Raccoon (short, low risk). Hold the Ice-Cream Rider until West has done at least two jobs. The Buck Stops Here opens by Day 20 at the latest.
- **Every quest ends.** Each quest's `end` line is its finish. When it is met, close it in the same GM-Δ, pay XP, write one line in `probation_file`.
- **Control-style site alerts.** Short, sudden, funny: a loose SCP-131 escorting the wrong person, a lockdown drill in the shower block, a stuck blast door Gus fixes with a kick. One scene each, never a quest.
- **Old-timer lore** (`development_threads.staff_lore`) is flavour, told by Gus, Dima and cafeteria staff. Those famous anomalies are not West's jobs this chapter.
- **Perks** give comfort and a good story, never a combat edge or a cure.
- **1999 joke.** Keep it light and constant. The truth (Incident 1999-EP-04) is dramatic irony; never push it at the player.
- **Day 90.** Run the review, then prompt: continue or extend (chapter rebase, engine §16.8). Never write "the end".
- **Fights.** Ducks: slapstick, painful, no lasting harm either way. Squirrels and pigeons: real fights at small scale. Deer: capture first (Ethics Committee rules); termination is allowed only for self-defence, collision cleanup, migration out of the area, or culling within state hunting quotas. Darrow's men: ordinary human fight, breakable.
