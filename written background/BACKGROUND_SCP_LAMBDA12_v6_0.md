# BACKGROUND — SCP: Class of '99 (Agent West) v6.0

For `NEW ENGINE v6.0`. **Round-0 truth**; once play begins this file is history and the Ledger changes instead. **Player: set only the `[UNASSIGNED]` entries before starting.** The GM withholds `locked_case_truths`, undiscovered NPC knowledge and hidden faction information during play.

**Undefined parts:** anything this background leaves undefined is the AI GM's to develop in play, consistent with its setting anchors, SCP canon and established facts (engine I3, §16.6). It is never a gap to wait on, refuse over, or expect the engine to fill.

Design: SCP Foundation, present day, played light. Ridiculous anomalies that make you sweat and think, real fights where the canon allows them, office comedy, staff lore and perks, slow-burn romance, and a rookie's life between jobs. **Not dark, not rushed into work, no world-ending plot.** Work arrives at a human pace with downtime between. Every quest has a clear finish; no quest is a 50-round investigation fog. Tone and structure partly borrowed from *Control* (a shifting HQ that opens up, side jobs that are rules-puzzles with a fight attached, sudden site alerts, deadpan memos). **No Control characters, names or places.**

**Changes in v5.6:** every mission is new. Different SCP entries, a new wrongdoer plot (Conrad Vale's perk-rental racket), and a new long main op (the lost survey team in SCP-3008). Leo's arc is new. The romance characters are re-tied to the new jobs. Rosa, Dima, the unit, the 1999 secret and the perks are unchanged.

### SCP primer for players new to SCP / 给不了解SCP的玩家的入门说明

*Player-safe: no spoilers. Read before starting. / 无剧透，开局前阅读。*

**What is SCP? / SCP是什么？**
SCP is a huge online shared-fiction project. In its world, a secret organisation called the **SCP Foundation** finds strange things that break the laws of nature (called **anomalies**), locks them away, and keeps the public from ever knowing. Its motto: **Secure. Contain. Protect.** Each anomaly gets a number (SCP-173, SCP-999…) and a file that reads like a dry government report — that dry tone is half the joke.
SCP是一个大型网络共创虚构项目。在这个世界里，一个叫 **SCP基金会** 的秘密组织负责寻找违反自然规律的怪东西（称为 **异常**），把它们关起来，并确保普通人永远不知道。座右铭：**控制、收容、保护**。每个异常都有编号（如SCP-173、SCP-999），并配一份像政府报告一样干巴巴的档案——这种一本正经的语气本身就是笑点的一半。

**In this game / 在本游戏中：** the Foundation hides behind a dull WHO programme, the "Special Containment Programme". You play **Agent West**, a brand-new field agent on 90 days' probation. The tone is light: weird jobs, office life, funny coworkers, some real fights, and maybe someone worth asking out.
基金会的公开身份是世卫组织下一个不起眼的"特殊收容计划"。你扮演刚入职、处于90天试用期的外勤特工 **韦斯特**。整体基调轻松：奇怪的任务、办公室日常、搞笑同事，偶尔有真正的战斗，也许还会遇到值得约会的人。

**Key words / 关键词**
- **Anomaly / 异常** — anything that breaks normal reality. / 任何违反正常现实的事物。
- **Containment / 收容** — keeping an anomaly locked up and secret. / 把异常关好并保密。
- **Object class / 项目等级** — how hard it is to keep in a box, *not* how dangerous it is. / 表示"多难关住"，*不是*"多危险"。
  - **Safe** — lock it in a box and it stays put. / 锁进盒子就老实待着。
  - **Euclid** — not sure what it will do; anything alive or thinking is at least Euclid. / 不确定会怎样；有生命或会思考的至少是Euclid级。
  - **Keter** — easily escapes the box. / 很容易逃出盒子。
  - **Thaumiel** — it *is* the box (used to contain other anomalies; top secret). / 它*就是*盒子（用来收容别的异常，绝密）。
- **Clearance Level / 权限等级** — what you are allowed to *know*, not your rank. West has Level 2. / 决定你能*知道*什么，不是职位高低。韦斯特是2级。
- **Field agent / 外勤特工** — the Foundation's eyes and ears: investigates, confirms, calls in the specialists. / 基金会的耳目：调查、确认，然后呼叫专业团队。
- **MTF (Mobile Task Force) / 机动特遣队** — specialist squads sent where needed. Each has a code and a nickname. / 派往各地的专业小队，每支都有代号和外号。
- **Amnestic / 记忆删除剂** — a drug that erases memories; used on witnesses. / 消除记忆的药物，用于目击者。
- **D-class / D级人员** — prisoners used for dangerous tests. / 用于危险测试的囚犯。
- **O5 Council / O5议会** — the 13 anonymous leaders at the very top. / 最高层的13位匿名领导。
- **Containment breach / 收容失效** — something got out. / 有东西跑出来了。
- **Groups of Interest / 相关组织** — outside groups that know about anomalies (dealers, artists, animal rescuers). / 知道异常存在的外部组织（商人、艺术团体、动物救援者等）。

**Your people / 你的人**
- **MTF Lambda-12 "Loose Ends" / 机动特遣队Lambda-12"收尾人"** — a small odd-jobs squad that ties up low-priority weird stuff worldwide: the jobs too small, too strange or too embarrassing for the big units. / 一支专门处理全球低优先级怪事的小队：那些对大部队来说太小、太怪或太丢人的活儿。
- **The 1999 joke / 1999梗** — the unit's veterans, born in the 1990s, call themselves the "Class of '99". They claim they secretly saved the world as kids in 1999 and swore never to tell, so everyone younger owes them coffee. West was born too late and never hears the end of it. / 队里的九零后老鸟自称"九九届"，坚称自己小时候在1999年偷偷拯救过世界并发誓保密，所以所有更年轻的人都欠他们一杯咖啡。韦斯特出生太晚，被调侃个没完。

**Staff perks / 员工福利** — some harmless Safe-class items can be booked for staff morale with **Form MU-2**, which takes a few days. / 一些无害的Safe级物品可以用 **MU-2表** 申请来提振士气，审批要几天。
- **SCP-999** — a friendly orange jelly blob that tickles and cheers people up. / 友善的橙色果冻团，会挠痒逗人开心。
- **SCP-294** — a coffee machine that pours any liquid you name (staff may only order normal drinks). / 能倒出你说出名字的任何液体的咖啡机（员工只能点正常饮料）。
- **SCP-458** — a pizza box that never runs out. / 永远吃不完的披萨盒。
- **SCP-261** — a vending machine with strange snacks, paid in Japanese yen. / 卖奇怪零食的自动售货机，要投日元。
- **SCP-131** — two little teardrop-shaped "eye pods" that follow people around like puppies. / 两个像小狗一样跟着人跑的水滴形"眼仔"。
- **SCP-529 "Josie"** — half a cat (only the front half), allowed to wander the site; she likes laps. / 只有前半截身体的猫，可以在站点里自由走动，喜欢趴在人腿上。
- **SCP-662** — a butler's hand bell; ring it and a perfectly polite butler, Mr. Deeds, appears to help. Booking strictly controlled. / 一个管家手铃，摇响后会出现一位彬彬有礼的管家"迪兹先生"来帮忙。使用受严格管控。

**Old-timer stories / 老员工的传说** — famous SCPs people joke about in the cafeteria. You won't be sent to these. / 食堂里大家拿来开玩笑的著名SCP，你不会被派去处理它们。
- **SCP-173** — a statue that moves when nobody is looking. "Don't blink." / 没人看时就会移动的雕像。"别眨眼。"
- **SCP-682** — a huge reptile that hates everything and cannot be killed. / 痛恨一切、杀不死的巨型爬行动物。
- **SCP-096** — "the Shy Guy"; seeing its face makes it hunt you. / "害羞的人"，看到它的脸它就会追杀你。
- **SCP-055** — something nobody can remember anything about. / 一个谁都记不住任何细节的东西。
- **SCP-914** — a clockwork machine that "refines" whatever you put in. / 能把放进去的东西"精炼"的发条机器。
- **SCP-500** — pills that cure any disease; never handed out. / 能治愈一切疾病的药丸，从不外发。
- **SCP-2521** — something that takes anyone who writes about it; its file is drawn in pictures. / 会带走任何用文字描述它的人的东西；它的档案全是图画。

**Anomalies you may meet on the job (no spoilers) / 工作中可能遇到的异常（无剧透）**
- **SCP-2006 "Too Spooky"** — a shapeshifter that desperately wants to be scary, and gets its ideas from cheesy old monster films. / 一个拼命想吓人的变形体，吓人点子全来自老掉牙的怪兽电影。
- **SCP-504 "Critical Tomatoes"** — tomatoes that fire themselves at anyone who tells a bad joke. The worse the joke, the harder they hit. / 会朝讲烂笑话的人发射自己的番茄。笑话越烂，打得越狠。
- **SCP-3008** — an ordinary-looking furniture superstore that is endless inside. Its faceless "staff" are harmless — until the lights go out. / 一家看似普通、内部却无限大的家具卖场。里面没有脸的"店员"平时无害——直到熄灯。
- **SCP-1507** — a flock of flamingos with a hive mind and a very bad temper. / 一群拥有蜂巢思维、脾气极差的火烈鸟。
- **SCP-5031 "Yet Another Murder Monster"** — a terrifying-looking monster that insists it must murder everyone… and mostly just wants a friend. / 一个外表恐怖、坚称自己必须杀光所有人的怪物……其实大多时候只想交个朋友。
- **SCP-1171 "Humans Go Home"** — something in an old farmhouse that talks by writing in the frost on the windows. / 老农舍里的某种东西，会在窗户的霜上写字跟人对话。
- **SCP-1867 "Lord Blackwood"** — a Victorian gentleman-adventurer with impossible stories, all told with total confidence. / 一位维多利亚时代的绅士冒险家，讲着不可能的故事，而且深信不疑。

### SCP logic the GM must keep (canon-checked)

```text
CLEARANCE ≠ RANK  levels 0–5 control what you may know, need-to-know only;
  field agents and most researchers are Level 2; MTF operatives and senior staff
  Level 3; Site Directors / MTF commanders Level 4; O5 Level 5. Having a level
  does not clear you for a project; being read in does.
FIELD AGENT  eyes and ears: undercover or embedded (police, hospitals, local
  services) or a suit who investigates. Confirms an anomaly, then calls a
  containment team. Not a soldier; may carry a sidearm.
WHO FIGHTS  security officers (site guards), tactical response teams, Mobile
  Task Forces (specialist units, often veterans). West fights when attached to
  them, when a breach reaches him, or in self-defence.
OBJECT CLASS = difficulty of containment, not danger. Safe: lock it in a box and
  it stays. Euclid: unsure (anything alive or sapient is at least Euclid). Keter:
  escapes the box easily. Thaumiel: is the box. Neutralized / Explained /
  Decommissioned as canon.
PERSONNEL CLASS  A (never near anomalies) · B (local essentials) · C (direct
  access to non-hostile anomalies; West) · D (expendable test subjects; treated
  as people, amnestics rather than casual death) · E (exposed, quarantined until
  cleared).
AMNESTICS  witnesses are given amnestics (Class A/B/C by strength); cover stories
  are routine (gas leaks, avian flu, renovation).
O5  thirteen members, numbered, never deal with day-to-day containment.
ETHICS COMMITTEE  real oversight: it limits what may be done to sapient or living
  anomalies (SCP-5031's treatment, SCP-1507 culls, SCP-3008 civilians).
WHO COVER  on paper the Foundation works through the WHO "Special Containment
  Programme" (gives the acronym and a reason to appear at odd outbreaks). WHO
  leadership knows only the cover. In practice the Foundation answers to the O5.
```

### Day 90 — chapter break, not an ending

West's probation review falls on Day 90 (`probation_review` quest). The review scene runs fully: the board weighs everything West actually did (every cover identity included), credit and grievance alike (AI_RULES ending rules). **The campaign does not end there.** After the scene, the GM prompts the player: *continue as is, or extend* — an extension is a new chapter built by the chapter rebase (engine §16.8) from this chapter's final state (new assignments, new SCPs, threads and relationships carried over). The 1999 folder may sit on the review table; opening it is the player's choice.

### Pressure and quest endings (setting-specific; engine §13.5 governs the rest)

- `vale_racket`: retires when Vale is exposed, caught, bought off, or flees; a bell lost to a buyer leaves a recoverable trail, never a lost cause.
- `team_birch`: retires when the survey team is out of SCP-3008, or settles with the enclave by choice; never "presumed dead" off-screen.
- `pink_flock`: local; retires when the flock is captured, relocated with Wilson's Wildlife Solutions, or scattered past the county line (Gamma-119 takes over).
- `rosa_transfer`: ordinary life; retires when the request is approved, withdrawn or refused.
- `elias_reassignment`: ordinary life; retires when Elias accepts, declines or is talked out of the Alaska post.
- Only `orientation_week` and `site_life` are quests at Round 0. Other quests open when Rosa assigns them (work source pace) or a site alert drags West in.

## Setting notes (GM-facing)

- **Pace.** Rosa assigns one job every 4–6 days; downtime between is real play (perks, Josie, lore, Leo's rehearsals, dates, site alerts). Never chain jobs back to back unless the player asks.
- **Quest order.** Any order fits. Suggested opening: Half a Cat (first week, plants the racket clue), then Critical Tomatoes (Day 14 is fixed), Frost Letters or Lord Blackwood. Hold Too Spooky and Murder Monster until West has done two jobs. Aisle Infinity opens by Day 20 at the latest. Pink Flock should open before Day 50 so Elias's Day 60 decision has weight.
- **The racket.** Vale is likeable on purpose. Seed clues gently (the crate, the flat bell, cold pizza, Gus's Friday van, Blackwood's story, Jun's party gossip). If West never notices, the gala happens and becomes the climax.
- **Every quest ends.** Each quest's `end` line is its finish. When it is met, close it in the same GM-Δ, pay XP, write one line in `probation_file`.
- **Romance.** Introduce all four options naturally by Day 10 (Priya Day 3, Sofia Day 4, Elias Day 6, Jun Day 9). Let the player choose who to spend time with; never force a love triangle. A rejected or ignored option stays a friend or colleague. Relationships carry over at the chapter break.
- **Control-style site alerts.** Short, sudden, funny: Josie asleep on a keyboard mid-report, a lockdown drill in the shower block, a stuck blast door Gus fixes with a kick. One scene each, never a quest.
- **Old-timer lore** (`development_threads.staff_lore`) is flavour, told by Gus, Dima and cafeteria staff. Those famous anomalies are not West's jobs this chapter.
- **Perks** give comfort and a good story, never a combat edge or a cure.
- **1999 joke.** Keep it light and constant. The truth (Incident 1999-EP-04) is dramatic irony; never push it at the player.
- **Day 90.** Run the review, then prompt: continue or extend (chapter rebase, engine §16.8). Never write "the end".
- **Fights.** Tomatoes: dodgeball with bruises. SCP-2006's stalker form: real close-combat danger, no deaths. SCP-3008 staff: real fights in the dark; light stops them instantly. Flamingos: a painful mob; capture first, Ethics rules on culls. SCP-5031: it panics, it does not attack — restraint, not combat. Thayne's guards: ordinary human fight, breakable.
