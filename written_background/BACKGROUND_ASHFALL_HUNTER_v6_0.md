# BACKGROUND — Ashfall: Hunter v6.0 (Combined Causal Test Variant)

For `NEW ENGINE v6.0`. Round-0 truth. Historical once play begins. Player: do not read.

**Undefined parts:** anything this background leaves undefined is the AI GM's to develop in play, consistent with its setting anchors and established facts (engine I3, §16.6) — never a gap to wait on, refuse over, or expect the engine to fill.

Design: Original-city demon-hunting action and investigation. Rin has a real shop, paying work and relationships; the Red Spur case intersects independent people and businesses, but nobody must meet, fight, help or survive for a planned scene. Leads point to people, demon traces, contested goods and hazardous places, not a mandatory records-desk itinerary.

```text
LAYER  PROBLEM                         MATERIAL CLUE / DANGER
1      Eli / Suds & Spin               shard, predatory lesser demons, compromised crew
2      charm supply and its victims    moving stock, living carriers, dangerous intermediaries
3      two accesses toward Red Spur    Halden 7-B OR Mercer pump / Southport culvert
4      2004 sealer and bloodline       Ada, surviving records, seal response, Court rivals
5      the route's fate                seal, bargain, isolation, collapse or other real outcome
Challenge bands live only in locations.<id>.challenge_band; this table holds none.

A: Eli's actual witnesses, phone and worksites can uncover the Halden route.
B: Gallo's stock, Suds & Spin residue, Jo's pursuit or a courier can expose the trade and Mercer runoff route.
C: Southport disturbances, pump workers and visible damage can reveal the Mercer access independently;
   municipal documents may corroborate facts but are not the sole path or an automatic solution.
Both entrances can be found, blocked, repaired or lost separately. Neither promises safe access or success.
```

Continuity: A stopped process stops its clock; a changed process retires or replaces that pressure rather than running the same harm twice. A successor exists only if a genuine cause survives. After the main arc's actual final mission/phase resolves, the city continues from the surviving state without an automatic new main quest or a fixed-round trigger.
